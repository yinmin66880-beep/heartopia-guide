Your First 7 Days

Just moved into town? Don't panic — follow this route for seven days: fewer early mistakes, no wasted coins. Every step is the optimal answer the community found the hard way.

DAY1

🗺️ Learn the map before settling in View the map · Claim resources

Open the menu at the top-right for the full map — spots with the bus icon are free fast-travel points. Grab the daily resources (Yo-yo Oak, Fluorite and stone spots) along the way; they reset at 06:00 every day, so it's free income.

DAY2

🛠️ Tools before the house Upgrade axe · rod · bug net

Upgrade your axe, fishing rod and bug net first — doubled gathering efficiency pays far better than rushing to hoard building materials. Whatever you save on day one, dump into tools on day two.

DAY3

🌾 Stagger your crops Mix different maturity times

Plant a mix of crops with different maturity times, so one finished batch never leaves you with a dead window of zero output. The wheat + pineapple rotation is a community-verified stable combo.

DAY4

📖 Collect encyclopedias along the way Catch every fish · net every bug

Fishing and bug encyclopedias award extra collection bonuses — do them passively as you travel. Check the weather calendar before heading out: rainy and rainbow days bring limited fish, don't miss them. The fishing encyclopedia lists every fish's conditions.

DAY5

💝 Get along with the villagers Supportive dialogue · remember birthdays

Pick supportive dialogue responses (like asking how the weather affects their hobby) — never neutral or negative options. Remember villagers' birthdays: a favorite gift on the day earns 3× favor.

DAY6

🍳 Unlock the cooking line Find Massimo · level with quick recipes

Head into town and find Massimo to unlock cooking. Level up with quick recipes first, and save key ingredients (frosting, rare fish) for high-value dishes — don't throw them into random stews.

DAY7

🏡 Plan your dream home Start small · follow a template

Never build a big house early! Start with an 8×10 cottage — flooring alone can drain your savings. Pick an F2P build from the template library and copy it, then upgrade as income flows in.

Pitfalls

Three Early-Game Traps

⚠️ Don't rush a big house — building materials cost far more than early-game income. An 8×10 start is safest; expand once your coin flow stabilizes.
⚠️ Don't decorate with overlap glitches — a patch can collapse the whole build. Only copy library templates tagged "no overlap glitches".
✅ Three things every day — redeem codes → gather resources → check the weather. 30 seconds, never a loss.

FAQ

Beginner FAQ

What should I do on day one in Heartopia?
Open the full map and learn the free fast-travel points, then claim the daily resources (reset at 06:00). Don't spend coins or materials freely — put everything into tool upgrades on day two; with doubled efficiency everything gets faster.
What's the fastest way to make money early?
Staggered farming (the wheat + pineapple rotation) as your baseline, plus selling high-price fish (European Lobster sells for 260, legendary fish even more). Check the weather before heading out — rainy and rainbow days double the payoff of limited fish.
What kind of house should a beginner build first?
Start with an 8×10 cottage. Better yet, pick a build from the F2P section of the template library — F2P builds use free or cheap in-game materials, and following the original tutorial's steps gets results without spending money.
What are the three daily must-dos?
Redeem codes (drop ~18:00, valid same-day) → gather daily resources (Yo-yo Oak / Flawless Fluorite, reset 06:00) → check the weather to plan fishing and gathering. The Daily Intel page shows it all on one screen.
