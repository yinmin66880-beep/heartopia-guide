FAQ

Fishing FAQ

What can I catch right now?
Use the "What's Biting Right Now" panel above: it follows your local time by default — just pick the weather to filter live. Weather should follow the weather calendar (official fixed schedule).
Which legendary fish can I catch on a rainbow day?
During the daytime: the Hammerhead Shark (Whale Sea, 600 coins) and Rainbow Trout (Lake, 180 coins). The European Eel, normally a rainy-night fish, also appears on rainbow-day evenings.
Where do I catch the Koi?
Only at the Secret Pond at night (00:00–06:00), and only on sunny days. Check the weather calendar to confirm tomorrow is sunny, then set an alarm.
Which fish sell for the most?
Legendary tier: Koi 800, Ice Sea Angel 750, Hammerhead Shark 600. High-value farmables include European Lobster 260 and Snow Crab 300. Rarity is marked on the top-right of each card.
