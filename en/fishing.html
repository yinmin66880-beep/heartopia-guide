Fishing Hobby Levels: Unlocks & Rewards

Fishing XP comes from unlocking new species — landing a fish you've never caught is what levels you up, so repeat-catching the same species earns very little. Higher levels unlock rarer fish and better tools.

5–20×🌈

The rainbow premium

On ordinary days the always-available base fish (Minnow 12 ~ Salmon 60) average only ~30 coins per cast. In the rainbow window, the Hammerhead is worth 20× that and the Rainbow Trout 6×. Same cast — pick the right window and the payoff multiplies.

140 coins🪱

The bait break-even line

Bait is crafted from apples, and each apple turned into jam is worth 67 coins — feeding bait to a 30-coin base fish just burns apples. Only windows where fish go for 140+ coins (Silver Chimaera / European Eel tier) justify bait; legendary windows always do.

Easy first🏆

Legendary camping order

Of the three legendaries, the Ice Sea Angel has the loosest conditions (snow, all day), the Koi is the pickiest (sunny late night only, plus finding the Secret Pond), and the Hammerhead is level-gated (Lv 10). Camp whichever legendary matches today's weather — never waste a snow day.

📊 Pair with the weather calendar — Rainbow and snow days are scarce in the schedule. Circle the next few days' windows on the weather calendar first, then plan your camping with the table above; on ordinary days just rotate spots to unlock new species instead of burning bait.
Technique

The Reel Battle: Read the Line Color

Fishing's core mechanic is the reel tug-of-war — the line color is your starting gun, and misreading it is the #1 reason big fish escape.

Safe⬜

White line

The line is slack and safe — hold to reel in continuously and bank the window.

Caution🟨

Yellow line

Tension is rising — ease off or tap lightly instead of holding. Don't get greedy.

Danger🟥

Red line

Release immediately! Keep pulling and the line snaps. Wait for white/yellow again.

Step1

Cast near the fish shadow

Spot a shadow in the water, drop your bobber next to it, and wait for the bite — a shake or exclamation mark means it's hooked.

Step2

Hold to reel, watch the color

Press and hold to start the battle. The color cycles white → yellow → red; whenever it turns red, let go.

Step3

Big shadows take patience

The bigger the shadow, the more tug cycles it takes. Apple-crafted bait and the fish lure (glowing blue shadows) both raise bite rate and quality.

Beyond daily selling, fishing has periodic contests and long-term collection goals with limited rewards.

Fishing Village⚓

How contests work

Contests open at the fishing-village boat: catch as many fish as you can within the time limit — personal goal 5 fish, team goal 30 combined, rewards include surprise gacha shells. Mermaid Perfume boosts efficiency for a short time.

Unlock 10 different species for the Bronze fishing medal, 50 for Silver, and the full encyclopedia for the Gold medal plus a special rod — the collector's Everest.

Value Chain

After the Catch: Sell or Cook?

Every catch has three fates: sell directly, cook for value, or donate to the encyclopedia. Good planners use all three.

FirstCatch

🐟 Donate new species first Encyclopedia rewards are hidden income

A species caught for the first time should go to the encyclopedia donation — the collection progress and achievements are income of their own. Worry about selling the duplicates.

AddValue

🍳 Cooking doubles fish value Raw → dish, higher margin

Many fish earn more as dishes: Salmon Sushi beats raw salmon by about 350 coins; 1-star fish under 130 coins suit Fish & Chips (with a 1-star potato); 2 shellfish + 2 fish make the Deluxe Seafood Platter. See the full recipe guide.

SellDirect

🛒 Legendary fish: sell as-is No hesitation at the top

Koi, Ice Sea Angel, Hammerhead Shark — the legendary tier sells fastest as-is: high unit price, no kitchen capacity needed. Let the stovetops chew through cheap low-star stock instead.

FAQ

Fishing FAQ

What can I catch right now?
Use the "What's Biting Right Now" panel above: it follows your local time by default — just pick the weather to filter live. Weather should follow the weather calendar (official fixed schedule).
Which legendary fish can I catch on a rainbow day?
During the daytime: the Hammerhead Shark (Whale Sea, 600 coins) and Rainbow Trout (Lake, 180 coins). The European Eel, normally a rainy-night fish, also appears on rainbow-day evenings. Note some rainbow-day species require a minimum fishing hobby level (see the unlock table above).
Where do I catch the Koi?
Only at the Secret Pond at night (00:00–06:00), and only on sunny days. Check the weather calendar to confirm tomorrow is sunny, then set an alarm.
Which fish sell for the most?
Legendary tier: Koi 800, Ice Sea Angel 750, Hammerhead Shark 600. High-value farmables include European Lobster 260 and Snow Crab 300. Rarity is marked on the top-right of each card.
How do I level fishing fastest?
XP comes mainly from unlocking new species — repeat catches earn little. Rotate spots, prioritize uncaught species, and blitz limited fish on special-weather days (they're all new species).
Why does my line keep snapping?
Snapped lines are almost always pulling through the red phase — release the instant it turns red and resume at white/yellow. Higher Rod Maintenance and Tactician levels also add tolerance.
When do fishing contests start and what do they reward?
Contests open at the fishing-village boat: catch within the time limit — personal goal 5, team 30 — for surprise gacha shells. Mermaid Perfume gives a short efficiency boost beforehand.
