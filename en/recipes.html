Basics

Cooking Mechanics in 4 Steps

Learn the rules before your kitchen loses money.

Step1

🔓 Unlock cooking Association Lv.5 + hobby ticket

At Residents' Association level 5, hand a Hobby Expansion Ticket to Massimo at the town center. Cooking caps at level 12, with recipes unlocking progressively.

Step2

🍳 Don't miss the quick-time prompt Miss = burnt

Cooking triggers a green shovel quick-reaction icon — tap it in time to protect quality; missing it turns the dish into a \"Strange Food / Drink\". Pure loss.

Step3

⭐ Star quality = price × energy Compare prices first

Ingredient stars directly raise the dish's sell price and energy restoration. But 2-star+ ingredients may sell better raw — compare before tossing them into the pot.

Step4

🔥 Run three stoves Output × 3

You can place 3 stoves side by side and cook simultaneously — an idle-farmer's dream. Paired with the ingredient supply from the coin route, that's triple throughput.

FAQ

Cooking FAQ

How do I unlock cooking?
Hand a Hobby Expansion Ticket to Massimo at the town center once your Residents' Association hits level 5. Cooking caps at level 12 with recipes unlocking along the way.
What's most profitable early on?
The jam → baking route: Mixed Jam (160) → Apple Jam (270) / Pineapple Jam (280) → Cheesecake (480); add Seafood Pizza (780) at level 4 for a big step up.
What does ingredient star quality do?
Stars raise the dish's sell price and energy — top-star dishes are worth multiples of 1-star. But 2-star+ ingredients may sell better raw; compare first.
Highest-profit late-game dish?
Steamed King Crab (Lv.10, 3000+ profit) > Camping Set (Lv.7, 1600+) > Cold Lobster Platter (Lv.8, 1000+). King crab needs the right weather window to gather.
