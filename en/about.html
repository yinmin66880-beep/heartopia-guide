About Us

Heartopia Guide (heartopiaguide.net) is an unofficial guide site built by Heartopia players — an automated data pipeline takes the "daily intel means digging through ten forum threads" problem off your hands, while the deep guides on fishing, decorating and making money do the heavy lifting.

Mission

The Problem We Solve

Heartopia's daily intel (codes, weather, resource spots) is scattered across different TapTap forum threads — players scroll through pages of replies every day just to piece together "what should I do today". This site aggregates it all into a live at-a-glance panel that updates itself daily, readable in the 30 seconds before you log in; alongside it sit deep tools like the fishing encyclopedia and home template library, so one lookup is all you need.

5 times daily🔄

Intel Updates Itself

The pipeline runs daily at 06:10 / 11:40 / 18:10 / 18:40 / 23:30 (Beijing time), scraping codes live the moment they drop.

Multi-source✅

Cross-Verification

Multiple community sources are scraped in parallel; only codes matched across sources earn the "verified" tag. Bad data never overwrites good data.

Transparent🔍

Traceable Data

How it updates

The Update Cadence at a Glance

Morning6

🌅 06:10 · Resource spots go live After the 06:00 in-game refresh

Yo-yo Oak / Flawless Fluorite refresh in-game at 06:00; the pipeline picks up the community-reported spots at 06:10, so early birds see them before logging in.

Midday12

🌤️ 11:40 · Midday check Catch-up scraping + corrections

The midday run catches anything the morning missed and compares the weather forecast against what actually happened — the calendar gets sharper every day.

Evening6

🎁 18:10 / 18:40 · Code scrapes Live the moment codes drop

Daily codes drop around 18:00; the pipeline's first scrape runs at 18:10 with an 18:40 safety net — even if the code post runs a few minutes late, the page has it by 18:40 at the latest.

Night11

🌙 23:30 · End-of-day sweep Same-day completeness check

The late-night run is the final safety net: anything still missing gets filled in, and the day's data is archived for the historical record.

Disclaimer & Contact

What's this site's relationship with the game's officials?
None. Heartopia Guide is a fan-built, unofficial community project with no affiliation or partnership with the game's developer XD Inc. or its publisher. The Heartopia name and all game assets belong to their original owners; this site only organizes guide-style write-ups within the bounds of fair quotation.
How is the site kept running?
Google AdSense display ads cover the domain and hosting costs — no paywall, no membership, every tool is free to use. How ad cookies are handled is explained in the privacy policy.
How do I report a data error?
If any page disagrees with what you see in-game (fish prices, appearance conditions, codes and so on), leave a correction under the source TapTap thread linked on that page — community feedback is a major channel for our revisions. The manually corrected entries in the weather calendar also come from player reports.
Why is it called a "handbook"?
The site's Chinese name (小镇攻略手账， "town guide handbook") borrows the image of the notebook a player carries around. We want this site to feel like a game handbook that writes itself for you every day — what to claim, what to catch, what to stock. Open it and the answers are right there.
