Mechanics

Gifting Rules in 4 Steps

Learn the rules first so no gift goes to waste.

Step1

💬 Daily chat unlocks gifting Talk first, gift later

Gifting unlocks after you chat with a resident daily and reach the \"Familiar\" favor stage. Talk to everyone early — the sooner it unlocks, the better.

Step2

🎁 Daily limits 1 per resident · 10 shared island-wide

Each resident accepts one gift per day, and all residents share a daily total of 10 gifts, resetting at midnight. Never repeat the same item to the same person in one day — they remember.

Step3

💕 Only gift \"Favorite\" tier +15–20 favor

Gifts fall into five tiers — favorite / liked / neutral / disliked / hated — with favor gains an order of magnitude apart. Cheat sheet below; dodging landmines matters as much as hitting favorites — a hated gift costs 15–20 favor at once.

Step4

💌 Chase the 5-heart event Letter → invitation story

At 5 hearts the resident sends a letter; reading it triggers their exclusive \"invitation\" story — Chuxia, Baili Ling, Ding Xiaokui, Gu He, Lan, and Fengxi each have one.

FAQ

Gifting FAQ

How do I unlock gifting?
Use the daily chat with a resident until favor reaches the \"Familiar\" stage. Chatting with everyone early is the fastest unlock.
How many gifts per day?
One per resident, 10 shared daily across all residents, reset at midnight; never repeat the same item same-day. Spend the 10 slots on \"favorite\"-tier residents first.
What raises favor fastest?
\"Favorite\" tier: +15–20, 5× the neutral tier. Zero-cost favorites: Lan·apples, Baili Ling·ores, Gu He·seeds/crops, Ding Xiaokui·animal feed.
What do 5 hearts give?
The resident mails you a letter; reading it triggers their exclusive \"invitation\" story — Chuxia, Baili Ling, Ding Xiaokui, Gu He, Lan, and Fengxi each have their own storyline.
