FAQ

Weather FAQ

Is Heartopia's weather random?
No. In-game weather follows an official fixed schedule — the whole server shares the same weather each day. The community turns it into a calendar (see the status bar for sources), so you can plan fishing and gathering in advance.
When is the next rainbow day? What can I catch then?
See the calendar above for upcoming rainbow days. During the daytime you can catch the Hammerhead Shark (600 coins) and Rainbow Trout (180 coins) — both normally unobtainable. Full conditions in the Fishing Encyclopedia.
What does "Sunny → Rain" (e.g. "Rain after 12:00") mean?
The first half of the day is sunny, then it rains from the stated time. Once it rains, rainy-day exclusives (Sand Bream, European Eel) become catchable — an afternoon trip pays best.
What are meteor showers for?
During meteor-shower windows (e.g. 18:00–24:00) you can gather meteorites at set spots — rare crafting/building materials. Missing one means waiting for the next scheduled window, so block out the time once the calendar confirms it.
