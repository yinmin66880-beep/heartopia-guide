Mechanics

How the Weather Refresh Works

Learn these three rules and you can plan fishing, gathering and stakeouts a day ahead of everyone else.

Rule1

🕐 Every day at 18:00, tomorrow's weather is set Server-wide

Heartopia's weather is not random — it follows an official fixed schedule shared across the whole server. Each day at 18:00 the next day's full weather is locked in, which is also why daily codes update at 18:00 — the code-drop posts double as weather announcements.

Rule2

⌚ See the next 7 days in-game Watch → Weather

Tap the Watch icon at the top-right → Weather tab to see today's and the next 7 days' weather and time slots (like "rain after 12:00"). Check after 18:00 and tomorrow's plan is confirmed.

Rule3

🌧️ Rain is often followed by a rainbow day Prep legendary fish early

Long-standing community observation: a rainy day is highly likely (not 100%) to be followed by a rainbow day. See rain today, and you can already line up tomorrow's Hammerhead Shark and Rainbow Trout route. No special-weather type repeats twice within one day.

Playbook

Special Weather Action Handbook

Every special weather brings exclusives: limited fish, limited gatherables, the traveling merchant Duoduo, and double gathering. Follow the handbook and no day goes to waste.

🌧️ Rainy day: free loot + double gathering

  • Limited fish: Northern Pike, Giant Pearl Mussel and Goldfish only bite in the rain; Octopus and Catfish are also more active on rainy nights
  • Duoduo's shop: on rainy days Duoduo sets up at the Art Street piano, selling umbrellas, candy, the Cloud Mountain Roll recipe, postcards and limited umbrella accessories
  • Double gathering: tree fruit, mushrooms and grasses yield ×2 in the rain — double the stocking efficiency
  • Birdwatching window: rainy days bring limited birds like the Blue-cheeked Bee-eater, Lesser Flamingo, Eastern Bluebird, Peregrine Falcon, White-headed Duck and Eurasian Eagle-Owl

🌈 Rainbow day: legendary-fish payday + double gathering

  • Limited fish: rainbow-day species unlock by fishing level (Lv. 6 Chum Salmon → Lv. 10 Hammerhead Shark, Swordfish) — the full unlock table is in the Fishing Encyclopedia
  • Rainbow bouquets: you can pick 3 Rainbow Bouquets that day and craft them into the high-value Rainbow Umbrella accessory — a hot market-stall item
  • Rainbow bubbles: a rainbow-day-only item that spawns randomly across the map
  • Duoduo's shop: on rainbow days Duoduo's spot isn't fixed — she randomly appears at one of Hot Spring Mountain, the Flower Fields, the Town Center, the Forest or Lighthouse Hill, selling rainbow candy, rainbow emote stickers and exclusive accessories
  • Double gathering: tree fruit, mushrooms and grasses ×2 — stock up like on a rainy day

☄️ Meteor shower: meteorite day (18:00 → next day 18:00)

  • Meteorite gathering: meteor showers usually run from 18:00 to the next day's 18:00, refreshing 9 meteorites across the map (white, yellow, purple and other colors), each staying put for 24 hours
  • Exchange chain: mine star-fall ore → collect star-fall shards → trade them to Duoduo for limited furniture and solo emotes
  • Wish making: make wishes during the shower, then trade with Duoduo for furniture and emote rewards
  • Hidden achievement: take photos together with a friend under the meteor shower to unlock a hidden achievement — miss it and you wait for the next window

❄️ Snowy day / ice season: frozen fun and limited fish

  • Limited fish: frozen waters open up — Snow Crab, Ice Sea Angel and the Ice-crystal series (Ice-crystal King Crab, Ice-crystal Pufferfish, Ice-crystal Seahorse, Ice-crystal Whale Shark) only appear during the ice season
  • Duoduo's shop: on snowy days Duoduo returns to Art Street, selling snow-day exclusives
  • Frozen fun: frozen-over lakes unlock ice activities; some pets only appear on snowy days between 00:00 and 12:00

FAQ

Weather FAQ

Is Heartopia's weather random?
No. In-game weather follows an official fixed schedule — the whole server shares the same weather each day. The community turns it into a calendar (see the status bar for sources), so you can plan fishing and gathering in advance.
When is the next rainbow day? What can I catch then?
See the calendar above for upcoming rainbow days. During the daytime you can catch the Hammerhead Shark (600 coins) and Rainbow Trout (180 coins) — both normally unobtainable. Full conditions in the Fishing Encyclopedia.
What does "Sunny → Rain" (e.g. "Rain after 12:00") mean?
The first half of the day is sunny, then it rains from the stated time. Once it rains, rainy-day exclusives (Sand Bream, European Eel) become catchable — an afternoon trip pays best.
What are meteor showers for?
During meteor-shower windows (e.g. 18:00–24:00) you can gather meteorites at set spots — rare crafting/building materials. Missing one means waiting for the next scheduled window, so block out the time once the calendar confirms it.
When does the weather update? Where can I see the 7-day forecast?
The next day's full weather is set each day at 18:00; in-game, tap the Watch → Weather at the top-right to see today's and the next 7 days' schedule. Check after 18:00 and tomorrow's plan is confirmed.
When does Duoduo (the limited merchant) appear? Where do I find her?
Only during special weather: on rainy days she is fixed at the Art Street piano (selling umbrellas, the Cloud Mountain Roll recipe and more); on rainbow days she randomly spawns at one of Hot Spring Mountain, the Flower Fields, the Town Center, the Forest or Lighthouse Hill; during meteor showers she handles star-fall shard exchanges; on snowy days she returns to Art Street.
Do rainy and rainbow days boost gathering?
Tree fruit, mushrooms and grasses gather at ×2 quantity on rain and rainbow days — the best window to stock decorating and cooking materials; rainbow days also let you pick 3 Rainbow Bouquets, craftable into the high-value Rainbow Umbrella accessory.
