Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026. This is a fan-made guide site made of fully static pages — we wrote this policy to be as readable as our game tutorials.

Overview

What do we collect?

Short answer: almost nothing. Heartopia Guide has no sign-up, no login, and no membership system. When you look up codes, check the weather, or browse the encyclopedias, we never ask you to provide any personal information — no names, no email addresses, no phone numbers.

✅ Every data tool on this site (codes / weather calendar / fishing encyclopedia / templates) works fully without cookies.
Analytics

Anonymous traffic stats

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate traffic (page views, visitor countries, device types) so we know which guides are most popular. It uses no cookies, no cross-site tracking, and no personal identification — the aggregated numbers cannot be traced back to any individual.

Ads

Advertising & Cookies

How are cookies used?
Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve interest-based ads based on your visits to this site and other websites, within the permissions you grant. These cookies are managed by Google — this site neither reads nor can read their contents.
How do I opt out of personalized ads?
Either option works: ① Visit Google Ads Settings and turn off ad personalization; ② Visit aboutads.info/choices to manage personalized ads from all participating vendors at once. You will still see ads afterward — they just won't be interest-based.
EEA / UK visitors
If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or another region with applicable regulations, you will first see a consent choice. Personalized ads are only shown if you accept; declining means you see non-personalized ads only.
Boundaries

Third-party links

Our guides quote and link to third-party websites such as Bilibili and TapTap. Once you follow those links you are on their sites — their data practices and privacy policies are independent of this site.

More

Children & policy changes

Children's privacy: This site is aimed at general players and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy updates: If this policy changes, we update this page and revise the effective date at the top. Continued use of the site means you accept the latest version.

Contact: Questions are welcome via GitHub Issues.
