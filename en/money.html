The Fast Coin Route

Four coin engines — daily freebies, fishing, farming and cooking — chained into one route you can run in 10 minutes a day. Fish prices come from our fishing encyclopedia and are fully verifiable.

Mainline

The Daily Coin Loop in 5 Steps

Run it in order and a stable daily coin income forms even in the early game.

STEP1

🎁 Daily freebies 30 sec · zero cost

Claim the redeem codes (updated 18:00, valid same day) + gather daily resources (Yo-yo Oak / Flawless Fluorite, reset 06:00). Whatever you don't have to buy is profit.

STEP2

🌾 Farming baseline Wheat × 2 rounds

Fill your fields with wheat in the morning (8h to mature, two rounds a day), then backfill with pineapple and tomato after harvest so plots never idle. Crops feed the kitchen — don't sell raw produce.

STEP3

🎣 Fish with the weather Rainbow day = jackpot window

Check the weather calendar before heading out: Sand Bream doubles its bite rate on rainy days, and rainbow days bring limited high-value fish like the Hammerhead Shark and Rainbow Trout. Full prices in the TOP10 table below.

STEP4

🍳 Cooking value-add Ingredients → dishes, profit doubled

Turn crops and catches into dishes before selling: 4 apples sell raw for 112, but one 1-star apple jam sells for 270 — the higher the star rating, the bigger the margin. Run three stoves at once for max throughput.

STEP5

🛒 Sell high Encyclopedia first

Donate every first-time catch for encyclopedia rewards first, then sell the duplicates — collection achievements are a hidden income stream of their own.

🌾 Stable cash flow

Crop Rotation: Never an Empty Plot

Farming is the lazy-friendly baseline engine — less explosive than fishing, but stable, and it keeps growing while you're offline.

MAINA

🌾 The wheat main loop 8h × 2 rounds

Wheat matures in 8 hours — plant in the morning, harvest again before bed for two stable rounds a day. Wheat is the base of apple pie and seafood pizza, so feed the kitchen first, sell the surplus.

FILLB

🍍 Pineapple / tomato backfill Zero dead time

Backfill the gap after each wheat harvest with pineapple or tomato so no plot sits idle. Mixing crops with staggered maturity is the community-verified "wheat + pineapple rotation".

PROC

🌷 Mutant flowers Gardening endgame

Once flowers unlock, planting red next to yellow raises the mutation chance, and mutant-color flowers sell for more — an extra profit engine for high gardening levels.

Pitfalls

Three Rules for Not Wasting Coins

⚠️ Tools before everything — upgrade the axe, fishing rod and bug net first for doubled gathering efficiency. Every coin saved on day one belongs in tools on day two.
⚠️ Don't rush a big house — building materials cost far more than early-game income. Start at 8×10 and expand once your coin flow stabilizes. For decorating, copy a build from the F2P section of the template library.

FAQ

Money FAQ

What's the fastest way to make money early?
A three-layer combo: claim the daily freebies first (codes + resources, 30 seconds); run two wheat rounds as your baseline; then cook ingredients into dishes — apples sell raw at 28 each, while 1-star jam sells at 270. Add high-price fish (European Lobster from 260) and the early coin flow is set.
Which fish are worth the most?
Koi 800 (Secret Pond · night · sunny) > Ice Sea Angel 750 (snowy) > Hammerhead Shark 600 (rainbow days, daytime). Rainbow days are the jackpot window: the Hammerhead Shark, Rainbow Trout and European Eel only appear then — check the weather calendar before heading out.
What's the most profitable crop?
The wheat + pineapple rotation: wheat matures in 8 hours for two rounds a day, with pineapple and tomato filling the gaps. Once gardening unlocks, plant red next to yellow for mutant flowers — higher trigger chance and higher prices.
How do I maximize cooking profit?
Star rating is the multiplier — a 5-star dish earns 10-70× the profit of its 1-star version, so save key ingredients for high-star dishes. Run three stoves at once; recipes follow your level: apple pie/mixed jam early → strawberry jam/seafood pizza mid → camping set/candlelight dinner/king crab dishes late.
